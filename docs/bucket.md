# Fishing Bucket

For Australians who fish from the shore. Not from a boat, and not for fish: for squid, cuttlefish, crabs, prawns and yabbies, the things you can walk to a pier or a sandflat and take home in a bucket.

It answers one question: when should I go, where, and what should I use?

## What it does

- Ranks the next seven days into time windows for squid, crab, prawn or yabby
- Logs what you caught, including the nights you got nothing
- Checks a photo of your catch against the size rule for that animal
- Shows your state's bag limits

Always check your state's current fishing rules before you keep a catch.
