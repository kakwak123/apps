# SingK (가왕각)

Sing a song and find out how good it really sounds. SingK listens to your recording and shows which of eight tiers your voice is at, from beginner up to star singer.

## What it does

- Scores your singing on pitch, resonance, long notes, breath and vibrato
- Shows which singers your voice sounds closest to
- Marks the moments that went wrong, so you can tap and hear them again
- Plays the same moment with the pitch corrected, so you can compare
- Tracks your scores over time

Scores are an AI estimate. They can change with your recording conditions and don't replace a teacher's opinion.

## Where it's up to

**Getting ready for Android testing.** The app runs on a real phone. Before release it still needs a public server, a closed test on Google Play and a final review. iOS comes after Android.
