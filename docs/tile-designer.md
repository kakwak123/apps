# Tile Floor Designer

Planning new tiles or flooring? Take a photo of your room and see what real tiles would look like there before you spend anything. Built first for homeowners in Melbourne.

## What it will do

- Show real, purchasable tiles laid in a photo of your room
- Work out how much you need and estimate the cost
- Connect you with a tiler nearby

## Where it's up to

**Early development.** The name may change, and the features above are the plan rather than a finished app.
